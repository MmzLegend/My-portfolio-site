Behind the work

The brief

Explore how virtual reality can be used to create gradual exposure to height within a research prototype.

My approach

I developed three environments: a rope bridge, a small building and a skyscraper. The project combined environment design with user interaction and questionnaire-based evaluation.

The work delivered

An interactive academic prototype and demonstration. It is not presented as a clinically validated treatment; the focus here is the design and development work.
